VI.

Frequently asked questions.

F9 or F9S?

The F9 is 3 kg lighter and easier for children and guests; the F9S has 40% more thrust, the Plus battery and stabiliser wings as standard. On a yacht with a tender, the F9S is the usual choice.

How long is delivery?

It depends on colour and available stock. Serial colours usually take weeks; custom paint a little longer. We confirm it in the quote.

Who provides the warranty?

The official SEABOB dealer, who invoices and delivers. Official service and spare parts.

Does AMARRA charge the customer a commission?

No. You pay the official SEABOB list price. AMARRA is an authorised sales agent and is paid by the official dealer.

Can I rent one with the boat?

Yes. If you are chartering with us, give us your dates and we will find a SEABOB at the base or include it in the quote.

What about the SE63?

A limited edition with deliberately restricted production. Send us your details and the official dealer confirms availability and allocation.
