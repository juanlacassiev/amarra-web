What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a site stores in your browser to remember something between one visit and the next. Some are essential for the page to work; others exist only to measure how it is used.

Cookies that are not necessary are not installed until you accept them. If you dismiss the notice without accepting, none are activated.

Today we use no advertising or profiling cookies for marketing purposes. If that changes we will update this page and ask for your consent again before installing them.

Changing your choice

You can review or withdraw your consent whenever you like from this site: open the cookie panel.

You can also delete or block cookies from your browser settings. Bear in mind that blocking the necessary ones may stop parts of the site from working.

If you want to opt out of Google Analytics specifically, across all sites, Google offers an opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

More information

How we handle data collected through cookies is explained in our privacy policy. The owner of the site and its contact details are set out in the legal notice.
